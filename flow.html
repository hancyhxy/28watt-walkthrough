一、整体分为 4 个阶段1. Four phases

A. 线下成交A. Offline sale

安装商上门、出报价，业主决定要装。不在平台上。Installer visits and quotes; homeowner decides to buy. Not on the platform.

B. 申请（C 端 + portal）B. Apply (homeowner + portal)

上传报价、AI 读取、填资料、比较方案。平台从这里开始，也是你的第一优先。Upload quote, AI extraction, basics, compare offers. The platform starts here; this is your first priority.

C. 审批（broker + portal）C. Approve (broker + portal)

补材料、broker 递交、贷款机构有条件批准。portal 要实时显示进度和缺什么。Documents, broker submission, conditional approval. The portal must show live status and what's missing.

D. 签约、安装、放款D. Sign, install, pay out

业主和贷款机构签合同，安装后放款给安装商，28Watt 拿佣金。大部分在贷款机构的系统里完成。Homeowner signs with the lender; funds go to the installer after install; 28Watt earns commission. Mostly happens in lender systems.

重点：阶段 B 和 C 里业主都还没签任何贷款合同，随时可能放弃。最容易流失的三个地方是：② 到 ④ 的交接（安装商发起后，业主要自己继续填）、④ 填收入的时候（担心隐私和信用分）、⑤ 补材料（找工资单、拍证件很麻烦）。你的设计提案就是针对这三处。Key point: In phases B and C the homeowner hasn't signed anything and can walk away at any time. The three biggest drop-off risks: the ②→④ handoff (installer starts, homeowner must continue alone), ④ income questions (privacy and credit-score worries), and ⑤ documents (finding payslips, photographing ID). The design proposal targets these three moments.
