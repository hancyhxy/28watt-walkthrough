核心观点The one idea

这不是一个表单，
而是一场接力。It's not a form.
It's a relay.

安装商发起，业主补完，贷款机构拿到完整材料才会批。任何一棒掉了，这单就没了。The installer starts it, the homeowner finishes it, and the lender needs a complete file. Drop the baton anywhere and the deal is gone.
